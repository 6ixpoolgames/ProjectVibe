# AI changelog

The AI is this folder (the context, sensing, memory, target selection, states and behaviours), the AI controller that acts for it (`controllers/AIController`), and its C++ side in `native/src/` (`AIMemoryStore`, `AIPawnGrid`, `AISensingJob`, `SightGeometry`, `ai_sight_rules.h`). Design: [docs/pawn/PAWN_SPEC.md](../../../docs/pawn/PAWN_SPEC.md) section 10, and [docs/scaling/SCALING_SPEC.md](../../../docs/scaling/SCALING_SPEC.md) sections 3 to 7 for the C++ and worker-thread parts. Overview: [README.md](README.md).

## 2026-11-23

- **Orders from elsewhere** (netcode spec 19.3 and 19.10): `AIContextComponent.order_source`, a static callable that, when set, decides each AI pawn's orders in its AI step instead of the AI; the AI's work moved from `run_ai` into `think`, which an order source may call. Played back from a recording, orders alone rebuild the game. Found on the way: the AI's death cleanup gives orders mid-tick, outside the AI step (a behaviour's exit); harmless, since a dead pawn's input is cleared, but the order stream's rule is that the AI gives orders only in its AI step.

## 2026-11-22

- **Owned tags** (tag spec step 2): the pawn owns its AI state's tag (`OwnedTags`). `AIStateBase.tag` sets it; empty, it's `ai.state.<node name in snake case>` (`get_tag`). The default states' tags are declared: `ai.state.idle`, `ai.state.alert`, `ai.state.combat`.

## 2026-11-19

- **Tells its pawn it's there** (state store step 3): its `_enter_tree` sets the pawn's reference to it, if the pawn has none yet, instead of the pawn looking through its children in `_ready`. The reference is there before any part's `_ready`.

- **`ai_sensing_job_selftest` sets each pawn's detectability through its stat, after it has its parts.** Before, it set `Pawn.detectability` before the pawn had its stats, so every pawn counted as 1 and the test never compared different values.
- **Detectability is read from the stat** (`StatManager.detectability_of`) by `SightSense` and `AIMemoryStoreGD`; `Pawn.detectability` is gone.

Tests: `projectile_selftest` part `keep_distance` (it closes from 18 m to about 10 and shoots, backs off from 6 m, melees at 2 m); `ai_batch_decider_selftest`'s real fight now counts Shoot or Attack, and Keep Distance or Chase, since the faction pawns mostly hang back now. - **C++ makes the decisions** (scaling spec 6.2, step 5c; the owner's call: C++ main, GDScript a dormant backup). `AIDecisionModule` (was `AIDecisionBatcher`, renamed: it no longer batches) is now in the project's module list (and `GameMode.DEFAULT_MODULES`), so every pawn decides in C++ unless it can't. It holds the C++ decider and each pawn's decision state there (`place`, `forget`), and makes each decision with one call (`decide_in_cpp`: the state's number, target, reason, the actions that could start, the inputs). The behaviour manager then only starts what C++ chose (`_switch_to`), and passes on the events C++'s copy must follow: a behaviour stopping on its own (`_stop_running`'s `tell_cpp`), persistence ticks and speed, jitter, interrupt thresholds. Records of C++ decisions mark the behaviours GDScript didn't work out (`RULE_NOT_WORKED_OUT`).
